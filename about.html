Who We Are

The Files Arts Project is a community-centered nonprofit dedicated to using the arts as a tool for education, leadership, connection, and creative expression.

Through workshops, events, mentorship, and arts-based programming, the organization helps create spaces where young people and community members can grow, perform, collaborate, and feel seen.

What We Value

Creativity

We believe the arts give people a powerful way to express themselves.

Community

We bring people together through shared experiences and cultural connection.

Leadership

We support young people as they build confidence, purpose, and voice.
